## Overview

Trialshopy is a smart AR shopping platform concept that combines augmented reality try-on, AI-powered product recommendations, and enterprise IoT analytics into a single retail technology suite. The platform is built to showcase how AR, AI, and IoT can come together to modernize enterprise retail operations.

## What Users Can Do

* **Virtual Try-On:** Visualize fashion, accessories, and other products in real time using AR before purchasing.
* **Explore AI Recommendations:** Discover personalized product suggestions driven by predictive analytics.
* **View Enterprise Solutions:** Learn about IoT and analytics integrations built for large-scale retail operations.
* **Browse Products:** Explore different product verticals like fashion e-commerce, accessories try-on, smart assistants, and 3D visualization.
* **Read FAQs:** Get quick answers about the platform's try-on speed, mobile SDK support, and 3D visualization capabilities.

## Why I Built This

I built Trialshopy to explore how AR and AI technologies can be packaged into a compelling enterprise-facing product page — the kind of platform real retail-tech companies use to pitch their capabilities to brands and enterprises.

The goal was to design and build a polished, multi-section marketing site that communicates a complex tech stack (AR, AI, IoT) in a way that's easy to understand for a non-technical enterprise audience, while keeping the frontend fast and responsive.
